R25ap updates fraction mass% least-squares fitting and its plots.

If your existing model is R25ao, copy only tdm_least_squares.py from this
package into your project folder, replacing its older copy. Keep your
tdm_inputs.json, tdm_batch_runs.csv, tdm_lsq_process_runs.csv, and
tdm_lsq_fraction_mass.csv. These files hold your model parameters, process
setup, and entered fraction analyses.

If your model is older than R25ao, use the six Python files listed in
UPDATE_EXISTING_PROJECT_R25AO.txt before running R25ap. Do not copy the
package's example input or run tables over your saved data.

Previously saved LSQ results do not gain the new residual or diagnostic
columns. Rerun LSQ to use this fix and produce updated reports.
